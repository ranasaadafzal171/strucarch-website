Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about construction claims and EOT in Pakistan.

What is the difference between a variation order and a claim?

A variation order is an instructed change to the works or rates agreed under the contract. A claim is a formal request for additional time or money arising from events under the contract, such as delays or changed conditions. Both need strong documentation. See our contractor billing services for the billing side.

What is an Extension of Time (EOT) claim?

An EOT claim is a request to extend the contractual completion date because of delay events that are not the contractor's responsibility. It requires evidence of the delay event, its timing and its impact on the completion date, usually supported by delay analysis.

What documents are needed to support a delay claim in Pakistan?

Typically: the contract and programme, delay notices and letters, site diaries and progress reports, meeting minutes, correspondence with the engineer or client, approved drawings, instructions, weather or utility records and any records showing when work actually happened.

Does Struc-Arch provide legal representation?

No. Struc-Arch provides engineering, quantity surveying and technical documentation services only. It does not give legal advice or represent parties in court. We prepare the technical record and work with lawyers where legal action is needed.

Who needs construction claims support?

Contractors, subcontractors, consultants and developers facing delayed payments, EOT disputes, measurement or rate disagreements, or claims by clients against them. Early documentation is usually the difference between a strong and a weak position.
