Site Inspection Checklist

A structured walk-through for a building project. Use it at each construction stage and for property inspections before purchase or renting.

STRUCARCH TEMPLATE · SAMPLE · REFERENCE FORMAT · NOT AN OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT FORM

SITE INSPECTION CHECKLIST

Inspection Date: ____   |   Stage: Foundation / Structure / Finishing / Handover

Mark each item: OK / DEFECT / N/A. Note the location and action required for every defect.

1. Site and ground

CheckResultRemarks
Site drainage and water stagnation around foundation  
Backfill compacted in proper layers and free of debris  
No cracks or settlement in adjacent structure  

2. Foundation and concrete

CheckResultRemarks
Excavation to correct depth and lines per drawing  
PCC bed laid on approved soil  
Reinforcement grade, size, spacing and cover per drawing  
Concrete mix, compaction and curing being done correctly  
No honeycombing, segregation or laitance issues  

3. Columns, beams and slabs

CheckResultRemarks
Column verticality, sizes and plumb within tolerance  
Beam and slab levels and thickness per drawing  
Lap lengths, stirrup spacing and clear cover correct  
No excessive cracks in RCC members  
Concrete compressive strength records available  

4. Brickwork and masonry

CheckResultRemarks
Wall thickness and bond pattern as specified  
Plumb and level of walls  
Mortar mix and thickness of joints uniform  
Damp proof course (DPC) at required level  
Lintels over openings with proper bearing  

5. Finishing and handover

CheckResultRemarks
Plaster and rendering sound, no hollow patches  
Roof waterproofing and drainage slope adequate  
Doors, windows and fixtures opening and closing properly  
Electrical and plumbing lines tested and working  
No signs of dampness, seepage or cracking in finished areas  

Summary

Attach dated photographs of each checked area. A checklist with photographs is evidence; a checklist alone is a claim.

When you need a professional opinion

  • Crack patterns that keep growing or follow a structural line
  • Deflection, sagging or leaning in any member
  • Before buying or renting a property
  • Before an extension, change of use or heavy loading
  • For a formal Building Fitness Certificate
