Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about BOQ preparation and estimation.

What does BOQ preparation include?

A full BOQ includes itemized quantities taken off from drawings, unit rates with rate analysis, material and labour schedules, and a summary with clear assumptions - delivered in editable Excel and PDF formats.

How much does BOQ preparation cost in Pakistan?

Cost depends on project size and complexity - typically a percentage of the project value for larger projects, or a fixed fee for homes and small buildings. Contact us with your project details for a free quotation.

Can you prepare a BOQ for my home construction?

Yes. We prepare detailed BOQs for residential construction so owners know material quantities and costs before work starts - helping avoid over-billing and shortages on site.

Do you provide BOQs for tenders and contractors?

Yes. We prepare tender-ready BOQs for contractors bidding on public and private works, including support for rate analysis and price schedules.

What is the difference between BOQ preparation and contractor billing?

BOQ preparation prices a project before construction. Contractor billing (IPC) documents completed work for payment during construction. See our Contractor Billing & IPC services for the billing side, our construction claims & EOT support for disputes, and the free BOQ and billing templates.
