Attach: measurement sheets, rate analyses for extra items, material on site statement, and this bill's progress photographs. Every quantity must trace to a signed measurement record.

What makes this bill pass the Engineer's review

  • Every quantity has a measurement sheet signed by both parties
  • Rates match the BOQ or have an approved rate analysis behind them
  • Deductions follow the contract clauses, not habit or memory
  • The bill is dated, numbered and submitted on time under the contract
  • Changed work carries a variation order or confirmed instruction
