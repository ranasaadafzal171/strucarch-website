Why case studies

Engineering is shown, not claimed

A building or a road is a record of decisions. These write-ups explain the reasoning behind those decisions, so clients and engineers can see how the work would be approached.

01

Honest scope

What was actually done, what the constraints were, and where the limits were.

02

Reusable method

The measurement, estimation and supervision steps apply to other projects of the same kind.

03

Respect for confidentiality

Client names, pricing and proprietary drawings are not published. Only the engineering approach is shared.
