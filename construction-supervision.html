Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about construction supervision in Pakistan.

What does construction supervision involve?

Construction supervision means checking on site that the work matches the drawings, specification and quality requirements - inspecting before and during each stage, checking materials and test results, recording progress, and catching defects early before they are covered up.

What is the difference between supervision and project management?

Supervision is site-focused: quality, measurement and conformance to drawings and specification. Project management is programme-focused: planning, scheduling, cost control, reporting and contract administration across the whole project. Struc-Arch provides both, which keeps site quality and project control aligned.

Do you supervise both buildings and infrastructure?

Yes. Struc-Arch has supervised buildings, schools and commercial structures, and infrastructure works including road dualization projects - most notably the Lilla Jhelum, Gujranwala-Hafizabad road and Gujranwala Expressway projects.

How do you report progress to owners?

Through regular progress reports covering actual against planned work, cost against budget, quality and test results, risks and issues, with supporting photographs and measurements. Reports are simple, factual and dated.

Can Struc-Arch take over supervision of an ongoing project?

Yes. Struc-Arch can take over an ongoing project by first auditing the current state - progress, quality, records and outstanding issues - and then establishing a clean baseline for supervision and reporting from that point.
