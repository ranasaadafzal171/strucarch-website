Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Struc-Arch Pakistan's engineering services.

What engineering services does Struc-Arch Pakistan provide?

Struc-Arch Pakistan provides structural design (ETABS, SAP2000, STAAD Pro), architectural design, building fitness certification, estimation and BOQ preparation, project management, construction supervision, and contractor billing support. The firm is based in Gujranwala, Punjab, Pakistan.

Where is Struc-Arch Pakistan located?

Struc-Arch Pakistan is located at Kacha, Eminabad Road, Gujranwala 52250, Punjab, Pakistan, and serves clients across Punjab and internationally.

Who is the founder of Struc-Arch Pakistan?

Saad Afzal is the founder and principal engineer. He is a Civil and Structural Engineer with a MSc in Structural Engineering, PMP certification and LEED Green Associate credential.

Does Struc-Arch Pakistan prepare BOQs and cost estimates?

Yes. The Estimation and BOQ package covers quantity takeoffs from drawings, itemised bills of quantities, rate analysis, cost estimates and tender documentation support.

Does Struc-Arch Pakistan help contractors with billing and IPCs?

Yes. Struc-Arch provides contractor billing and technical documentation support, including measurement, Interim Payment Certificate (IPC) preparation, BOQ reconciliation and claims documentation. Contact Struc-Arch to discuss your project.
