Variation Order Template

A change that is not confirmed in writing is a change you may never be paid for. This reference format records the scope, the price basis and the time impact before the work is done.

Attach rate analysis where the rate is not from the BOQ. Confirm the change in writing before starting the work wherever possible.

When to use this form

  • An instruction changes the drawings, quantities or specifications
  • Extra or non-schedule items are introduced on site
  • Work is omitted, and the contract allows a deduction for the omission
  • The change also affects the programme, so an EOT notice is triggered

Full process explained in contractor billing & IPC and claims & EOT documentation. Price new items with the rate analysis and calculator tools.
