What is an IPC (Interim Payment Certificate)?

An IPC is the certified statement of the value of work executed in a given period, prepared against the BOQ and contract, including additions, deductions, retention and prior payments. Contractors are paid against approved IPCs - accurate preparation matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about contractor billing in Pakistan.

Who needs contractor billing and IPC services?

Contractors, subcontractors, builders and construction companies who need accurate running bills, Interim Payment Certificates, measurement sheets and claims documentation - especially where consultants or clients require professional documentation standards.

Where does Struc-Arch provide these services?

Struc-Arch is based in Gujranwala, Punjab, and supports clients across Pakistan - including Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Sialkot, Gujrat and Faisalabad - plus international contractors.

What documents are needed for contractor billing?

Typically: the signed contract and BOQ, approved drawings, measurement sheets, site records, previous IPCs, rate analyses and any variation orders. Struc-Arch helps you organise these into a clean billing file.

What is the difference between a variation order and a claim?

A variation order is an instructed change to the works or rates agreed under the contract. A claim is a formal request for additional time or money arising from events under the contract - such as delays or changed conditions. Both need strong documentation.

Is Struc-Arch a law firm?

No. Struc-Arch provides engineering, quantity surveying and technical documentation services. It does not provide legal advice or court representation.
