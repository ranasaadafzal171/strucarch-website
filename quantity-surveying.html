How it works

From drawings to final account

01

Quantify

Quantities are taken off from the drawings or model in a clear, itemized schedule.

02

Price

Rates are built from materials, labour and plant with rate analysis, and assembled into a BOQ or estimate.

03

Measure

As work proceeds, actual quantities are measured and turned into running bills and valuations.

04

Settle

Final quantities, variations and rates are reconciled into the final account - and defended if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about quantity surveying in Pakistan.

What does a quantity surveyor do?

A quantity surveyor measures and values construction work: quantities from drawings, bills of quantities, measurement of work done, valuations and payment certificates, and cost advice. The work covers the whole project, from tender to final account.

What is the difference between a quantity surveyor and an engineer?

The engineer designs and builds the works. The quantity surveyor measures and prices them - quantities, BOQs, measurements, valuations and cost control. Struc-Arch provides both under one roof, which keeps the numbers consistent with the design.

Do you provide quantity surveying services for home construction?

Yes. For homes, Struc-Arch prepares material takeoffs and cost estimates so owners know quantities and costs before work starts, and measures work and prepares running bills during construction.

Can a quantity surveyor help with tenders?

Yes. Struc-Arch prepares tender-ready BOQs, rate analyses and priced schedules for contractors bidding on public and private works, and reviews tenders for clients who need to check rates and quantities.

What is re-measurement in construction?

Re-measurement is measuring the work actually done against the contract BOQ and adjusting the bill to actual quantities. It is the basis of running bills and the final account on item-rate contracts.
