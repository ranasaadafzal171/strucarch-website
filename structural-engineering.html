Structural Engineering Services

Complete structural design, analysis, drawings and inspection for residential, commercial and infrastructure projects - delivered by a structural engineer with a MSc in Structural Engineering, PMP and LEED Green Associate credentials.

What we do

From concept to code-compliant structure

Every structure begins as a diagram. Struc-Arch turns those diagrams into safe, buildable, code-compliant buildings and infrastructure - with 2,000+ buildings delivered under load, on schedule and on code.

Structural Design & Analysis

Modelling, analysis and design of RCC and steel structures using ETABS, SAP2000, STAAD Pro and SAFE.

Structural Drawings (GFC)

AutoCAD structural drawings - general arrangement, foundation, beam, slab, column and detailing.

Structural Inspection & Audit

Structural stability assessment, load verification, remedial recommendations and fitness certification.

Specifications & BOQ

Technical specifications, bills of quantities and material schedules to accompany design and drawings.

Design Reports & Approvals

Complete design calculation reports and support for municipal, PEC and client approvals.

Seismic & Foundation Design

Seismic design considerations and foundation design for safe performance in Pakistan's seismic zones.

Who leads the structural engineering work?

Saad Afzal, Founder & Principal Engineer - Civil & Structural Engineer with a MSc in Structural Engineering, PMP certification and LEED Green Associate credential.

Which software and codes do you use?

ETABS, SAP2000, STAAD Pro and SAFE for analysis and design, with AutoCAD for structural drawings. Designs are carried out in line with applicable codes (ACI / UBC / IBC-based practice) and client requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about structural engineering services.

What structural engineering services does Struc-Arch provide?

Struc-Arch provides structural design and analysis (ETABS, SAP2000, STAAD Pro), AutoCAD structural drawings (GFC), specifications and BOQs, design reports and approvals support, structural inspection and building fitness certification, and seismic/foundation design.

Where does Struc-Arch provide structural engineering services?

Based in Gujranwala, Punjab, Struc-Arch serves clients across Pakistan - including Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Sialkot, Gujrat and Faisalabad - plus international clients.

What types of buildings do you design?

Residential homes and villas, commercial buildings and plazas, schools and institutions, industrial facilities and infrastructure projects.

Do you provide structural drawings for construction?

Yes. Every design includes AutoCAD structural drawings (GFC) covering general arrangement, foundation, beam, slab, column and detailing - ready for construction and approval.

Can you inspect and certify an existing building?

Yes. Struc-Arch performs structural inspection, load verification, remedial recommendations and building fitness certification. See our guide to building fitness certification in Punjab.

Related services: BOQ preparation & cost estimation, contractor billing & IPC preparation and construction claims & EOT support. Also see the structural engineering guide.

Send your drawings or site details for a free preliminary review. We will recommend the right scope and give you a fixed quote.
