Engineering Glossary

Civil and structural engineering terms used on real sites in Pakistan - in plain language, with the standards where they matter. Part of the Struc-Arch Knowledge Base.

Contracts & Billing

Terms contractors and engineers meet first

BOQ (Bill of Quantities)

A priced or unpriced list of all measured work items in a project, with units, quantities and rates. It is the basis of tendering, valuations and final payment.

BBS (Bar Bending Schedule)

A table showing every reinforcing bar in a member - diameter, shape, length, bending and quantity. Used for cutting, bending and steel measurement. Related: structural knowledge.

IPC (Interim Payment Certificate)

The certificate the Engineer issues for the value of work done in a period, after deductions for advances, retention and prior payments. See contractor billing.

Running Bill

The contractor's periodic (usually monthly) statement of measured work, rates and deductions submitted for certification. Basis: measured quantities × rates.

EOT (Extension of Time)

Extra contract time granted when delays are not the contractor's fault - bad weather, late drawings, variations, or employer-caused suspension. See claims & EOT.

Variation Order

A written instruction changing the scope, quantity, quality or timing of the work, with an agreed price or rate adjustment. Template: variation order.

Retention

A percentage (often 5-10%) deducted from each payment and released after defects liability, protecting the employer against uncorrected defects.

FIDIC

The international standard forms of contract published by the FIDIC federation, widely used for government and donor-funded works in Pakistan, often alongside PEC forms.

PEC

Pakistan Engineering Council - the statutory regulator that registers engineers and engineering firms in Pakistan. A PEC registration number appears on certified work.

PPRA

Public Procurement Regulatory Authority - the rules that govern how public-sector contracts are advertised, tendered and awarded in Pakistan.

Measurement Book (MB)

The site record where measured quantities of executed work are entered for permanent record and used to prepare bills. Template: measurement sheet.

Rate Analysis

Working out the cost of one unit of work - material, labour, plant, overheads and profit - to fix or verify a rate. Try the BOQ & rate analysis tool.

Structures

How structures behave

Bearing Capacity

The maximum soil pressure a foundation can safely support without excessive settlement or shear failure. It decides the foundation type and size.

Allowable Bearing Pressure

The working (serviceability) soil pressure used in design - the ultimate bearing capacity divided by a safety factor, or from the soil report.

Dead Load

Permanent weight - self weight of slabs, beams, columns, walls and finishes. It usually dominates a building design. See structural knowledge.

Live Load

The movable load - people, furniture, vehicles, stored goods. Codes give minimums by occupancy (residential, offices, halls, parking).

Deflection

The vertical displacement of a member under load. Excessive deflection causes cracking and poor serviceability even when strength is safe. Formula: δ = 5wL⁴/384EI (UDL, simply supported).

Moment of Inertia (I)

A measure of a cross-section's resistance to bending. For a rectangle: I = bh³/12. Bigger I means stiffer member. Try the section properties tool.

Section Modulus (S)

Resistance to bending stress at the extreme fibre: S = I / y. Bending stress = M/S. For a rectangle: S = bh²/6.

Slenderness Ratio

The effective length of a column divided by its least radius of gyration. High slenderness means buckling governs before material strength.

RCC (Reinforced Cement Concrete)

Concrete takes compression, embedded steel bars take tension. The workhorse of buildings in Pakistan. Steel grade and concrete strength are set by design.

Cover

Distance from the outer concrete face to the nearest reinforcement, protecting steel from fire and corrosion. Required minimums are set by codes.

Grade Beam / Plinth Beam

A beam at plinth level tying columns together, supporting walls and distributing loads to foundations while reducing differential settlement.

Shear Wall

A vertical concrete wall that resists lateral (earthquake and wind) forces. Essential for mid- and high-rise seismic design in Pakistan.

Civil & Infrastructure

Earth, roads, water

Earthwork

Cutting and filling of soil to shape the ground. Volume is computed between cross-sections (average end area method: V = L × (A1 + A2)/2).

Compaction

Mechanical densification of soil to increase strength and reduce settlement. Measured by field density tests against a standard (Proctor) density.

Subgrade

The prepared natural soil on which a road or pavement is built. Its strength largely controls pavement thickness.

Pavement

The layered structure carrying traffic - subbase, base course and wearing surface (flexible/bituminous or rigid/concrete).

Culvert

A small structure that lets water pass under a road - pipe, box or slab type. Often a hidden source of under-design if hydrology is skipped.

Retaining Wall

A structure holding back earth or water at different levels, resisting lateral soil pressure by its weight (gravity), friction (cantilever) or reinforcement (RCC).

Wet Volume vs Dry Volume

Wet volume is the placed concrete volume; dry volume (×1.54) accounts for voids and shrinkage when batching materials. See concrete mix tool.

W/C Ratio

Water-cement ratio - the single biggest control on concrete strength and durability. Lower ratio = stronger, less workable concrete.

Proctor Test

The laboratory test establishing the maximum dry density and optimum moisture content for compacting a given soil.

NDT (Non-Destructive Testing)

Testing that does not damage the member - rebound hammer, ultrasonic pulse, cover meter - used in building fitness certification.

As-Built Drawing

Drawings updated to show what was actually constructed, including every field change. Required for handover and future maintenance.

DRA (Data Retrieval & Application)

The complete document file assembled for school fitness and safety certification applications in Punjab - see school certification.

Units & Measurements

Numbers used on Pakistani sites

Marla

Standard land unit in Pakistan: 1 marla = 272.25 sq ft = 25.29 m². A kanal = 20 marla. Convert with the unit converter.

Kanal

20 marla = 5,445 sq ft = 505.86 m². Used for plots and land in Punjab and beyond.

Cft (Cubic Foot)

Unit of volume (1 cft = 0.0283 m³) still widely used for sand, aggregate and brickwork quantities in Pakistan.

sqft to sqm

Area conversion: 1 m² = 10.764 sq ft; 1 sq ft = 0.0929 m². See the unit converter tool.

MPa vs psi

Concrete strength units: 1 MPa = 145 psi; 1 ksi = 6.895 MPa. C-20 concrete ≈ 20 MPa ≈ 2,900 psi.

kN·m

Kilonewton-metre - the moment (torque) unit used in structural design in Pakistan (SI). 1 kN·m = 0.7376 kip·ft.

Grade of Concrete (C / M)

C = characteristic strength in MPa (e.g. C-25). M = mix proportion (e.g. M15 = 1:2:4). Both are used; never confuse them.

Steel Weight (d²/162)

Weight per metre of a bar in kg: d² / 162 (d in mm). 12 mm bar ≈ 0.888 kg/m. Try the rebar weight tool.

Abutment

The end support of a bridge that carries the deck and retains the approach embankment. Seen on the Lilla-Jhelum dualization and Gujranwala road schemes. Related: Lilla-Jhelum case study.

Anchor Bolt

A steel bolt embedded in concrete that fixes a column base plate or machine foundation. Size, embedment and edge distance come from the base plate design.

Axial Load

A load acting along the longitudinal axis of a member - compression in columns, tension in hangers. Columns are checked for axial load plus moment (P-M interaction).

Asphalt

The bituminous wearing course on a road. Thickness and mix are designed from traffic loading; 25-50 mm is common for the surface course on secondary roads.

Bending Moment

The internal moment that causes a member to bend. It drives flexural steel in beams and slabs; units are kN·m. Related: structural knowledge.

Shear Force

The internal force acting transverse to a member's axis, causing it to slide or crack diagonally. Beams and slabs need shear checks in addition to bending.

Critical Path

The longest chain of dependent activities in a programme - it sets the project duration. Delays on the critical path extend the completion date; EOT claims depend on it.

Curing

Keeping fresh concrete moist so it gains its design strength. In Pakistan's heat, curing starts immediately after finishing and continues 7-14 days; skipping it causes shrinkage cracks.

Escalation

A contract price adjustment for changes in material, labour or fuel costs during the works. Calculated with an agreed formula; not automatic - it needs a contract clause. Related: how to calculate escalation.

Final Bill

The final statement of account issued at practical completion, covering all certified work, variations, retention release and adjustments. It closes out the contract. Related: IPC vs final bill.

Formwork

The temporary mould (timber or steel) that shapes fresh concrete until it hardens. Formwork cost and reuse are major items in BOQ estimation.

Liquidated Damages

A pre-agreed daily sum the contractor pays for late completion, written into the contract. If the delay is the employer's fault, the contractor can claim EOT to escape them.

RFI (Request for Information)

A formal written query from a contractor to the engineer asking for clarification, approval or missing information. RFIs are dated and recorded - they support delay and variation claims.

Site Diary

A daily factual record of site events - weather, labour, plant, instructions, delays and visitors. It is contemporaneous evidence in claims and disputes. Related: daily report template.

Subcontractor Bill

A payment certificate or running bill issued by a main contractor to a subcontractor for measured work done. Measurement and withholding rules mirror the main contract.

BOQ Reconciliation

Comparing billed quantities against the BOQ to identify over- or under-measurement, omissions and rate mismatches before payment. Core to contractor billing.
