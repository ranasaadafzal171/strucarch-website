FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from clients across Pakistan and Asia.

Is Struc-Arch considered one of the best structural engineering consultancies in Pakistan and Asia?

Yes, with over 15 years of experience, 2,000+ buildings delivered, and major infrastructure projects like the Lilla Jhelum Dualization (128 km), Struc-Arch Pakistan is recognized as a top-tier civil and structural engineering consultancy in the region, offering best-in-class structural design, estimation, and project management services.

What credentials does the principal engineer hold?

Saad Afzal is a PEC-registered engineer, holding a BSc in Civil Engineering (UET Lahore) and an MSc in Structural Engineering (UET Taxila). He is also a certified Project Management Professional (PMP) and a LEED Green Associate.

What software does Struc-Arch use for structural analysis?

We use industry-standard software including ETABS, SAP2000, STAAD Pro, and AutoCAD for comprehensive structural analysis, finite element modeling, and architectural drafting.

Do you offer construction claims and contractor billing services?

Yes, we provide expert contractor billing, Interim Payment Certificates (IPC), BOQ reconciliation, delay analysis, Extension of Time (EOT), and technical claim reports.
