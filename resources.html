How to use these

Three rules before you download and print

Reference formats only work when you adapt them to your own contract.

1. Match the contract

Your contract, the BOQ and the particular conditions of contract may specify exact forms and formats. Where they do, use theirs. Where they do not, these reference formats fill the gap.

2. Fill in real detail

A template with empty cells is not a bill. Every quantity needs a measurement sheet behind it, every rate a basis, every deduction a contract reference. That detail is what survives an audit.

3. Keep the evidence

The document itself is only half the record. Keep the measurement sheets, photographs, site instructions and correspondence that support every number you claim.
