Rule of thumb: measure together, agree on site, and sign on the spot. Corrections should be struck through, initialled and dated, never erased.

Good measurement habits

  • Measure jointly with the Engineer and sign immediately
  • Record dimensions at site, not from memory back at the office
  • Match every line to a BOQ item number and its unit
  • Keep a running total per item across the project
  • Photograph the measured work the same day
