Building Fitness Certification

Structural and hygienic fitness assessment and certification for schools, hospitals, banks and commercial buildings in Pakistan - with complete file preparation and application support. Over 1,588 school structures certified.

Fitness certification

Confirm that a building is safe for continued use

A fitness certificate is not paperwork. A registered engineer forms a professional opinion about the actual condition of the building - and stands behind it.

Structural Assessment

Visual inspection and load verification of the structure - cracks, corrosion, spalling, settlement, member sizes and capacity against the intended use.

Hygienic Fitness

Assessment of premises conditions - safe, clean and serviceable accommodation - alongside the structural opinion for school certification.

DRA File Preparation

Complete, correct building files - drawings, approvals, structural records and reports - ready for submission to the relevant department.

SIS Application Support

Assistance with online application submission on the School Information System (SIS), so the process moves, not stalls.

Remedial Recommendations

When defects are found, clear, prioritised guidance on repairs, strengthening or load reduction - with costs and sequencing.

Certificate & Reports

A clear fitness certificate and assessment report - what is sound, what needs attention, and what the professional opinion is.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about building fitness certification in Pakistan.

What is building fitness certification?

Building fitness certification is the process of confirming that a structure is safe for continued use. A registered engineer assesses the structural condition of the building and issues a professional opinion on its fitness for occupancy and use.

Who needs a fitness certificate?

Schools and institutions in Punjab are routinely required to hold fitness certification by provincial education and works departments. Hospitals, banks, government buildings and commercial properties also request certification for compliance, banking and insurance purposes.

What does the assessment cover?

The assessment covers the structural condition of the building - visible cracks and their patterns, corrosion and spalling, settlement and alignment, member sizes and load capacity - plus the hygienic fitness of the premises, and the completeness of the building file.

How long does certification take?

It depends on the size and condition of the building and the state of the records. A simple school building with complete records can be assessed and documented quickly; buildings with missing records or defects take longer because they need additional verification or remedial work.

Does Struc-Arch prepare the SIS and DRA application?

Yes. Struc-Arch handles the full workflow for schools: structural and hygienic fitness certification, complete DRA file preparation, and assistance with the online application submission on the School Information System (SIS).
