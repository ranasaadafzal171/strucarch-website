Building Handover Checklist

The formal handover is when the building becomes the owner's responsibility. Walk through it properly, snag what is not right, and collect every document.

STRUCARCH TEMPLATE · SAMPLE · REFERENCE FORMAT · NOT AN OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT FORM

BUILDING HANDOVER CHECKLIST

Project: ____   |   Handover date: ____   |   Contractor: ____   |   Owner: ____

Mark each item: OK / SNAG / N/A. Every SNAG goes on the snagging list with a location and a fix-by date.

1. Structure and building fabric

CheckResultRemarks
No significant structural cracks or settlement  
Roof watertight; drains and falls clear  
No dampness or seepage in walls or floors  
External walls, render and finishes sound  

2. Finishing and fittings

CheckResultRemarks
Plaster, flooring and paint complete and sound  
Doors, windows, locks and hardware operate correctly  
Sanitary fixtures fitted, watertight and draining  
Built-in furniture and kitchen units complete  

3. Services and testing

CheckResultRemarks
Electrical circuits tested, labeled and safe  
Water supply working; plumbing pressure-tested  
Gas line connected and checked by the licensed utility  
All fittings, switches, fans and lights operational  

4. Documents and keys

CheckResultRemarks
As-built drawings handed over  
Test certificates, warranties and guarantees included  
Utility connections, bills and approvals transferred  
All keys, remotes and access cards handed over  
Completion certificate issued or arranged

A handover without a signed snagging list is a handover without protection. Photograph every item and keep a copy with both parties.

Why a proper handover matters

  • It fixes responsibility for defects before final payment
  • Documents make the building maintainable, saleable and insurable
  • Test certificates prove services are safe to use
  • A signed handover ends the contractor's snagging obligations cleanly

For schools and public buildings, handover often leads to a Building Fitness Certificate. Struc-Arch inspects completed buildings and certifies fitness across Punjab.
