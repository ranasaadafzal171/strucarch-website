House Construction Quality Checklist

A stage-by-stage checklist for building a house in Pakistan. Check each stage before the next one starts, and never let new work hide an earlier defect.

STRUCARCH TEMPLATE · SAMPLE · REFERENCE FORMAT · NOT AN OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT FORM

HOUSE CONSTRUCTION QUALITY CHECKLIST

Plot / House: ____   |   Owner: ____   |   Date started: ____

Mark each item: OK / DEFECT / N/A. Note the location and action required for every defect.

1. Drawings and approvals

CheckResultRemarks
Approved structural and architectural drawings on site  
Municipal / Tehsil Municipal Administration approval obtained  
Setback, floor area and height per approval  
Soil condition known (soil test done or engineer advised)  
Construction sequence and concrete mixes agreed in writing  

2. Earthwork and foundation

CheckResultRemarks
Excavation to correct depth, width and line per drawing  
PCC bed laid on sound, approved soil  
Rebar size, spacing, cover and laps per structural drawing  
Concrete mix, compaction and curing correct  
No honeycombing, segregation or exposed rebar  

3. Structure: columns, beams and slabs

CheckResultRemarks
Column positions, sizes and plumb per drawing  
Slab and beam levels, thickness and cover correct  
Lap lengths, stirrup spacing and clear cover correct  
Chairs and spacers used; steel not resting on earth  
Concrete compacted, no cold joints; curing started on time  
Formwork removed only after concrete has gained strength  

4. Brickwork and masonry

CheckResultRemarks
Bricks sound, uniform and soaked before laying  
Wall plumb, level and true to line; joints uniform  
Mortar mix and workmanship correct  
Damp proof course at the required level  
Lintels with proper bearing over all openings  

5. Waterproofing and services

CheckResultRemarks
Roof waterproofing laid properly with correct falls  
Bathrooms and wet areas tanked / waterproofed  
Electrical conduits, boxes and wiring sizes per design  
Plumbing pipes pressure-tested before covering  
Gas lines installed by the licensed utility / contractor  

6. Finishing and handover

CheckResultRemarks
Plaster sound, no hollow patches; surfaces plumb  
No dampness, seepage or cracking in finished areas  
Doors, windows, locks and fixtures operate correctly  
Electrical board and circuits tested and labeled  
Sanitary fixtures watertight and draining well  
As-built drawings, keys and warranties handed over

When to call in an engineer

  • Before the foundation steel is placed
  • Before each slab is cast
  • When cracks or settlement appear
  • Before buying or renting an existing house
  • For a Building Fitness Certificate at completion
