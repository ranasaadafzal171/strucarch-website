Contractor Claim Template

A claim that is not documented on time is a claim that may be lost. This reference format structures the entitlement, the event, the delay and cost impact, and the evidence, so the claim is professional and defensible.

This is a technical documentation reference format. It is not legal advice. Where legal representation is required, engage a qualified legal professional separately.

When to use this form

  • A delay event affects the completion date and an EOT is warranted
  • Additional cost arises from an event that is not the contractor's fault
  • A payment or quantity dispute needs a structured, evidenced submission
  • A deadline in the contract requires a claim to be submitted on time
