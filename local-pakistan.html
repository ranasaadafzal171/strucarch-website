Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about engineering services across Pakistan.

Do you provide structural engineering services across all of Pakistan?

Yes. Struc-Arch is a PEC-registered civil and structural engineering consultancy based in Gujranwala that works across every province and region of Pakistan - Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Islamabad Capital Territory, Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan.

How does Struc-Arch serve cities far from Gujranwala?

Design, estimation, billing and certification work is largely delivered remotely, with scheduled site visits for inspection, supervision and certification. This is how Struc-Arch has delivered 2,000+ buildings and certified 1,588 school structures across Pakistan.

Is Struc-Arch PEC registered?

Yes. Struc-Arch is a PEC-registered firm (licence #Consult/2452) and principal engineer Saad Afzal holds PEC registration #Civil/32786, with a BSc in Civil Engineering (UET Lahore) and an MSc in Structural Engineering (UET Taxila).

Which provinces and cities does Struc-Arch cover?

Struc-Arch covers all of Pakistan. This page lists dedicated pages for every district-headquarters city - see the province sections below for Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Islamabad Capital Territory, Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan.

What services do you provide across Pakistan?

Structural design and GFC drawings, BOQ and cost estimation, quantity surveying, contractor billing and IPC, building fitness certification, claims and EOT support, and construction supervision - for residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects.
