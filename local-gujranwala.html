Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about engineering services in Gujranwala and Punjab.

Who is the best structural engineer in Gujranwala?

Struc-Arch is a PEC-registered civil and structural engineering consultancy (firm licence #Consult/2452, principal engineer Saad Afzal PEC #Civil/32786) based in Gujranwala at Kacha, Eminabad Road. Do you provide house structural design and GFC drawings (naqsha) in Gujranwala?

Yes. Struc-Arch prepares structural design and GFC drawings for houses, shops, plazas and factories in Gujranwala - the engineering behind a complete house naqsha - sized for local soils and loads, drawn to satisfy the Gujranwala Development Authority (GDA) approval process, and priced at a fixed quotation.

Do you provide structural engineering services in Gujranwala?

Yes. Struc-Arch is based in Gujranwala (Kacha, Eminabad Road) and provides structural design, BOQ and cost estimation, quantity surveying, contractor billing, building fitness certification and supervision for projects in the city and across Punjab.

How much does structural design cost for a house in Gujranwala?

Cost depends on the size and complexity of the house, the number of storeys, and the extent of drawings and calculations. Struc-Arch gives a fixed quotation after reviewing the plan, so owners know the price before work starts.

Can you help contractors in Gujranwala with running bills and IPCs?

Yes. Struc-Arch prepares measurement sheets, running bills and Interim Payment Certificates (IPCs) for contractors working on government and private projects across Punjab, including market-rate data relevant to the region.

Do you provide building fitness certification for schools in Punjab?

Yes. Struc-Arch has certified 1,588 school structures across Punjab, including structural and hygienic fitness certification, DRA file preparation and support with the School Information System (SIS) application.

What road and highway projects has Struc-Arch worked on in Punjab?
