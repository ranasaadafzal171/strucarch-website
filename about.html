Saad Afzal

Engineering Systems Expert & Founder, Struc-Arch Pakistan. Based in Gujranwala, connecting design, cost and delivery into one workflow - with growing international work.

Professional Profile

From diagram to delivered structure.

Saad Afzal is a civil and structural engineer with over 15 years of practice across structural design and analysis (SAP2000, ETABS, STAAD Pro, AutoCAD), estimation, and project management. He has delivered more than 2,000 buildings - including 1,588 school structures assessed and certified in the first half of a single year alone - working with public-sector departments, housing authorities and private clients across Punjab.

As Founder of Struc-Arch Pakistan, he leads a team of engineers, architects, project managers and consultants providing structural and architectural design, stability analysis and certification, and delivery consulting for residential, commercial, industrial and civic works.

On the mega-project side, he has contributed to province-scale highway delivery - the at-grade dualization of the Lilla Jhelum corridor (128 km), the dualization of the Gujranwala - Hafizabad road (74.15 km) and the grade-separated Gujranwala Expressway (15.2 km) - alongside more than 2,000 buildings assessed, certified and delivered.

  • MSc Structural Engineering - UET Taxila, 2017
