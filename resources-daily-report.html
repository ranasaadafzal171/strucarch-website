Attach 2-3 dated photographs. Record facts, not opinions, and never go back and rewrite an old report.

The report that protects you

  • It is the factual basis of your monthly running bill quantities
  • It records delays and their causes for an extension of time claim
  • It captures instructions before they become a dispute about what was said
  • It is accepted evidence in measurement, delay and payment disputes
